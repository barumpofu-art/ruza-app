Ruza Budget Tracker

An Android budgeting app for Botswana. It works out your spending by reading the transaction messages Orange Money, MyZaka, Access Bank, Absa and FNB already send you — so it starts with months of your real spending instead of an empty ledger.

Nothing leaves your phone

The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission. Without it Android will not let the app open a network connection, so it cannot send your messages or your spending anywhere. No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking.
