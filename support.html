A transaction is missing

The app can only see what a provider texts you. If a payment sent no message, add it by hand with the + button — cash and anything else that leaves no trace belongs there too.

If a message did arrive and the app did not pick it up, it may be a wording the app has not learned yet. Those messages are kept: open Settings → Re-check stored messages after an update and the app will try again on everything it previously could not read.

An amount looks wrong

A reversed or mistaken transaction can be excluded from your totals without being deleted: tap it in the history and choose to exclude it. Deleting is deliberately not offered, because the message is still in your inbox and the row would come straight back the next time the app scanned.

The app is not finding anything at all

  • Check SMS permission is granted: Android Settings → Apps → Ruza Budget Tracker → Permissions.
  • The app reads messages from Orange Money, MyZaka, Access Bank, Absa and FNB. Messages from other providers are not recognised yet — email me the wording, with the amounts and any names or numbers removed, and I will add it.

Moving to a new phone

Use Settings → Export backup on the old phone and Import backup on the new one. Because nothing is stored anywhere but the phone, there is no account that carries your history across for you — the backup file is the only way.

Deleting your data

Everything is on your device. Clear it through Android Settings → Apps → Ruza Budget Tracker → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing to ask me to delete.
