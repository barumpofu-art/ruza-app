Privacy Policy — Ruza Budget Tracker

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Ruza Budget Tracker ("the app") is an Android app that works out your spending by reading the transaction text messages your mobile money services and banks send you. This policy explains exactly what the app reads, what it keeps, and where that information goes.

The short version: nothing leaves your phone. The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission. Without it, the operating system will not allow the app to open a network connection, so it is not capable of transmitting your messages, your transactions or anything else to us or to anyone else. There is no account to create, no server to sign in to, and no analytics.

Who is responsible

The app is developed and published by an independent developer. For any question about this policy or about your data, contact: barumpofu@gmail.com.

What the app reads

With your permission, the app reads SMS messages already stored in your phone's inbox, and messages as they arrive. It does this to find records of money moving in and out of your accounts.

It looks for messages from these senders:

  • Orange Money
  • MyZaka
  • Access Bank
  • Absa
  • FNB

To find messages from a provider that has changed its sender name, the app also scans the newest messages in your inbox generally. Messages that are not financial are examined only long enough to determine that, and are not recorded — see the next section.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app stores is written to private storage on your device, in an area that Android does not make available to other apps. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Messages that arrive while the app is closed

So that a transaction is not missed when many messages arrive between uses, the app notices incoming SMS as they arrive. A message is written to a temporary file in the app's private storage only if it could be financial — that is, if it comes from one of the senders listed above, or if its text names a pula amount or one of those providers.

Every other message is discarded the moment it arrives and is never written down. A message from a friend or family member does not reach the app's storage at all.

The temporary file is processed and deleted the next time you open the app. Of what was in it, only messages from the financial senders listed above are kept beyond that point.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send your messages, transactions or balances anywhere.
  • It does not contain advertising.
  • It does not contain analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not share or sell any information to anyone, because it holds nothing outside your device and cannot transmit.
  • It does not send SMS messages, and does not read your call log or contacts.
  • It does not ask for your PIN, password or any account credential, and it cannot move money.

When you choose to export

The app can export your transactions as a CSV or a JSON backup file. This happens only when you ask for it. The file is handed to Android's standard share screen, and you choose where it goes — saving it to your device, emailing it, or sending it to another app. Once you send a file to another app or service, that file is subject to the privacy practices of whatever you sent it to, not this one.

Permissions the app requests, and why

PermissionWhy it is needed
READ_SMS To read transaction messages already in your inbox, which is how the app can show you months of real spending immediately rather than asking you to type it in
RECEIVE_SMS To notice a transaction message as it arrives, so a busy inbox does not push it out of reach before you next open the app
USE_BIOMETRIC Only if you turn on the optional app lock. This shows your device's own fingerprint, face or PIN prompt. The app never receives your fingerprint, face data or PIN — only whether your device confirmed it was you.

You may refuse SMS access, or withdraw it later in Android's Settings → Apps → Ruza Budget Tracker → Permissions. Without it the app cannot find your transactions automatically, though anything you entered by hand remains.

How long information is kept, and how to delete it

Information stays on your device until you remove it. You are in control of all of it:

  • Delete individual records, or exclude them from your totals, inside the app.
  • Clear the stored unreadable messages from the app's settings.
  • Clear everything at once through Android's Settings → Apps → Ruza Budget Tracker → Storage → Clear data.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Because nothing is ever sent to us, there is no copy anywhere for us to delete on your behalf, and no request you need to make to us to have it removed.

Children

The app is intended for people managing their own money and is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of your information changes, this policy will be updated before or alongside that release, and the date at the top will change. In particular, if a future version ever required network access, that would be stated here plainly and the claim above about the app being unable to transmit would be corrected.
